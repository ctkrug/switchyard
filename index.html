A train shunting puzzle that never runs out

Switchyard is a train shunting puzzle game you play in the browser. A short train of mixed cars waits on the lead track, each siding wants one specific car, and you throw switches to steer every car home in as few moves as possible. Unlike a fixed level pack, every yard is generated the moment you load it and checked by a solver before you see it, so the puzzle is always new and always fair.

How a yard works

Cars queue on the lead track, front car first. A switch thrown to the right diverts the front car into that switch's siding; left keeps it running down the line. One throw moves at most one car, so the challenge is ordering your throws to send each car to the right place without misrouting the ones behind it. Every yard reports a par, the shortest solution the generator's own solver found, and your best result against par is saved between sessions.

Questions

Is Switchyard a free railway puzzle browser game?
Yes. It runs entirely in your browser with no sign-up, no install, and no backend. Open the page and a yard is waiting.
How is each logic puzzle train game generated?
A constraint generator builds the track topology and the car manifest together, then a breadth-first solver searches for a solution. If one exists, the yard ships and its move count becomes par; if not, the generator tries again. You only ever see solvable yards.
Does the train shunting puzzle ever repeat?
Each yard is seeded at load time, so the odds of replaying one are tiny. Hit "New Yard" any time for a fresh board, and difficulty widens (more cars, more switches) as you clear yards in a session.
Can I play the railway puzzle on my phone?
Yes. The board scales to any screen, the switch levers are large touch targets, and animation respects your reduced-motion setting.
What if I misroute a car?
Use Undo to step back one throw, or Reset to send the whole yard back to the start. Nothing is ever lost to a misclick.
